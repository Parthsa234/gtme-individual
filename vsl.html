02 The problem

Traditional outbound wasn't built for the AI era.

Most outbound still runs on manual research, copy-pasted templates and tools that don't talk to each other. The work is slow, the messages sound the same, and doing more of it means hiring more people. Flip the switch.

Prospect research ✓
Done by hand

Hours on LinkedIn and company sites, one account at a time.

Signals in a table

An ICP written as signals and run as a Clay table with an enrichment waterfall.

Tools ✓
Disconnected

Data in one tab, copy in another, replies in an inbox nobody owns.

One connected system

Each machine feeds the next: list, research agent, sequencer, replies, dashboard.

Outreach ✓
Repetitive

The same template sent to everyone, rewritten by hand when it stops working.

Three plain-text steps per segment, tested against reply rate.

Personalisation ✓
Surface-level

Written from evidence

Workflow ✓

An agent that reads before it writes.

M4 · Copy & cadence

Three steps. Plain text. Tested.

A three-step sequence by segment on day 0, 3 and 7, sent from warmed mailboxes with LinkedIn alongside, and judged by one thing: reply rate.

  • Trigger line first, one line of pain
  • No link in step one
  • Your first campaign goes live on day 5
Tools · EmailBison, Smartlead, Instantly, HeyReach
M5 · Reply & booking

Never let a "yes" go cold.

Replies are classified automatically, a first response is drafted, positives are routed to a human in minutes, and calls get booked with a clean CRM hand-off.

  • Reply classification
  • AI first response, human hand-off
  • Speed rules that hold
Tools · n8n, HubSpot
M6 · Dashboard

Know what to keep and what to kill.

The weekly scoreboard: sent, replies and opportunities by segment, read from the sequencer and the CRM. New segments and angles come from the numbers, not gut feel.

  • What to count, what to cut
  • Read live during sessions
  • The view a buyer actually reads

Q.01What is GTM engineering?+

GTM (go-to-market) engineering is building the systems that find a company's buyers, research them and start the conversation. In practice it's six machines: sending infrastructure, a list engine, an AI research agent, copy and cadence, reply handling, and a dashboard. A GTM engineer owns all six, end to end.

Q.02Do I need coding experience?+

No. The machines are built in Clay, Claude and a sequencer. Where a script helps, you get the script and we show you what it does.

Q.03Is it live or recorded?+

The builds and the 1:1s are live, with your work on screen. Recordings stay in the vault so you can rebuild anything later.

Q.04Is this suitable for career switchers?+

Yes, if you can give it 6–10 hours a week. You leave with real campaigns and their numbers to show, which is more useful than a certificate.

We can't promise a job, and nobody honest can. What we can show you is what people built and what those machines produced.

Q.05What will the tools cost me?+

Week one gives you a cost sheet for your own sending volume before you buy anything, so you don't overpay for tools. Many students start on what their company already pays for.

Q.06What is AI outbound?+

Outbound where AI does the research and first drafts at scale: an agent reads each account's evidence and writes the opening line, and automation sorts the replies. People still decide who to target, what to say and how to answer.

Q.07What technologies will I learn?+

Clay, Claude, Apollo, Prospeo, MillionVerifier, Apify, EmailBison, Smartlead, Instantly, HeyReach, n8n, GoHighLevel, HubSpot and Inboxkit, plus Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for mailboxes. Each one is taught inside the machine it serves, and the core tools are covered for the length of the program.

Q.08How practical is the program?+

Very. Your first campaign goes live on day 5, lists are built with you, and you learn on a live account under supervision. Sessions are live, never pre-recorded clicks.

Q.09Who should apply?+

SDRs, BDRs and AEs who want to build rather than copy-paste; founders running their own pipeline; freelancers and agency owners; RevOps and growth operators; and career switchers who can commit 6–10 hours a week.

Q.10How can I speak with the GTME.School team?+

Apply above and, if it looks like a fit, you can book a 30-minute call straight away. Or write to hello@gtme.school; replies 10:00–22:00 IST.
